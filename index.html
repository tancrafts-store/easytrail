Privacy note

Your key is stored only in this browser (localStorage), and your photos are sent directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API. Easy Trial has no server.

A key kept in browser storage is not protected like a server-side secret and can be read by anyone using this browser. Use a personal key with usage limits, and don't share this app with a key you want to keep private.
